# OSU AI Assistant Documentation

## Project Overview
The OSU AI Assistant is a chatbot designed to help Oregon State University students access information about various aspects of campus life and academics. The assistant provides real-time information about academic calendars, course details, campus activities, and student services.

## Features

### 1. Academic Information
- **Academic Calendar**
  - Term start and end dates
  - Final exam schedules
  - Holiday breaks
  - Registration deadlines
  - Drop/add deadlines

- **Course Information**
  - Course descriptions
  - Prerequisites
  - Available sections
  - Professor information
  - Class locations and times
  - Bacc core requirements

### 2. Campus Life
- **Housing & Dining**
  - Dorm information and amenities
  - Dining hall locations and hours
  - Meal plan options
  - Housing application deadlines
  - Special dietary accommodations

- **Student Organizations**
  - Club listings and descriptions
  - Meeting times and locations
  - Contact information
  - How to join/start a club
  - Upcoming events

### 3. Academic Support
- **Library Services**
  - Valley Library hours
  - Study room reservations
  - Research assistance
  - Computer lab availability
  - Special collections access

- **Academic Advising**
  - Advisor contact information
  - College-specific advising
  - Walk-in hours
  - Appointment scheduling
  - Major exploration resources
